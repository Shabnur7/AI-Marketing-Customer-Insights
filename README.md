
# AI Marketing Customer Insights

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates a data-driven marketing workflow for a wireless earbuds product.

Customer reviews were analyzed using Python to identify product strengths, customer pain points, marketing opportunities, and product improvement areas.

The project also automates the creation of social media content and a marketing campaign concept based on customer feedback.

## Business Problem

Marketing teams need to understand what customers value and what problems they experience before creating campaigns.

The goal of this project is to transform raw customer reviews into actionable marketing insights.

## Key Findings

### Customer Performance

- Total reviews: 8
- Average rating: 3.88/5
- Strength-oriented reviews: 62.5%
- Needs improvement: 25.0%
- Pain point reviews: 12.5%

### Top Customer Strengths

1. Sound quality
2. Comfort and lightweight design
3. Value for money

### Main Customer Concerns

1. Battery life
2. Connectivity
3. Microphone performance
4. Charging case quality

## Marketing Strategy

The analysis suggests positioning the product around:

- Great sound quality
- Lightweight and comfortable design
- Everyday value

The campaign concept developed from these findings is:

**Everyday Sound, Everyday Comfort**

## Social Media Content

The project automatically generates:

- Instagram caption
- Social media hooks
- Product tagline

The content is based on validated customer strengths rather than unsupported product claims.

## Product Improvement Recommendations

Customer feedback suggests focusing on:

- Battery performance
- Bluetooth connectivity
- Microphone clarity
- Charging case quality
